# Forecast evaluation

Generated by `train.py` on 2026-10-31. Network 14→32→32→4×4
(one softmax per horizon), 2064 weights; validation loss 1.0768.

Zambretti letters are read as A–F fair, G–M cloudy, N–X rain, Y–Z storm, as shipped in the app. For
reference, the row "tuned cut points" uses the boundaries that score best on the training years
(B, E, O), which no longer agree with the letters' texts.

Task: weather in the next 3, 6, 12, 24 h at the watch's location, worst condition wins:
STORM (gusts ≥ 60 km/h or ≥ 8 mm in 3 h), RAIN (≥ 1.0 mm, snow included),
CLOUDY (mean cloud cover ≥ 70 %), otherwise FAIR.

Data: hourly ERA5 reanalysis via the Open-Meteo archive, 55 places. Trained on 2019–2022,
early stopping on 2023, tested on 2024. The unseen places (kassel, kitzbuehel, krakow) were never trained on.
Test inputs carry 0.15 hPa of sensor noise. "Wet" means RAIN or STORM. Zambretti has no horizon of its own,
so its single forecast is scored against each horizon.
